# The challenges

The three challenge sheets are in `challenges/`. They're based on real challenges from the Scottish Government's CivTech programme. We aren't entering CivTech.

**Every squad must get the same picture**, so present them from the sheets, not in your own words.

## Picking a challenge (about 20 minutes, together)

This is a together moment. If several people in the squad run this at once, suggest they gather round one screen (the merge captain's or the product owner's) instead. A remote squad shares a screen on Teams.

1. **One line:** these are based on real Scottish Government challenges, any squad can take any of them, and several squads can pick the same one.
2. **Each challenge in the same shape**, one at a time or side by side, using the sheet's own headings and wording, shortened only by leaving bullets out:
   - The problem (two or three lines)
   - Who's affected
   - What gets in the way
   - What people need
   - Things to keep in mind
3. **Where the squad's mix fits.** Read the professions in `TEAM.md` and give one short line per challenge, for example "your user researcher and service designer know this kind of journey". Never say a squad can't take a challenge because it lacks a profession: everyone can do more than their job title today.
4. **Invite questions.** "Ask me anything about these before you choose." Answer from the sheets first. If you go beyond them, say where the answer comes from (the CivTech page, or a search) and give the source.
5. **Hand the choice back.** Ask the squad to talk it over. Two questions help: "Which problem do you care most about?" and "Which of these people would you most like to help?" They can dot-vote on the printed sheets if they prefer.

## Don't

- **Don't suggest what to build** at this stage. Choosing and understanding the problem matters more than solutions. If someone is stuck for ideas and asks, you can brainstorm a few, but say they're only examples.
- **Don't recommend a challenge** unless asked.
- Don't add facts that aren't in the sheets or a source you can name.

## Recording the choice

- **Only in the product owner's session.** Once the squad has agreed, ask which challenge as clickable options (the three challenges, single choice), then write it into `TEAM.md` with one or two sentences on why, and `/share` it.
- **In anyone else's session:** say "Your product owner records it. Once they have, say 'get the latest'." Don't write it yourself, so two people never record the same decision.

**The squad name** is recorded the same way: whenever the squad picks one (any time before 17:15), the product owner's session writes it into `TEAM.md` and shares it.

Then the squad talks it through (see "The day" in `CLAUDE.md`). Before the day, help anyone read and ask about the challenges, but leave the choice for the day.
