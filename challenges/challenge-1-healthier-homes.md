# Challenge 1: Healthier homes

## Finding tenants at risk before they get ill

Based on [CivTech Challenge 12.6](https://www.civtech.scot/civtech-12-challenges), sponsored by Cloch Housing Association.

### The problem

Cold homes, damp and mould are making social housing tenants in Scotland ill. Fuel poverty means some tenants can't afford to heat or ventilate their homes properly, even when the home itself is in good condition. Cloch Housing Association in Inverclyde wants to find the tenants most at risk and help them before harm happens. Today, Cloch mostly finds out about problems when tenants report them. New Scottish rules, including Awaab's Law, mean landlords must investigate and fix damp and mould faster.

### Who's affected

- **Tenants**, especially older people, people with long-term breathing (respiratory) or other health conditions, young children and people on low incomes
- **Cloch's staff**: housing officers, repairs teams, and the energy advice and community support teams
- **Health services**, which feel the pressure when people get ill
- **The housing regulator**, which checks landlords meet the rules

### What gets in the way

- Help usually comes after a problem is reported, so after harm has started.
- The information Cloch has (property details, repairs history, some tenant information, sensors in a small pilot) isn't joined up.
- There's little usable information about who has health conditions, so it's hard to know who's most vulnerable.
- Putting sensors in every home costs too much.
- A modern, energy-efficient home can still be a risk if the tenant can't afford to heat it.
- Some tenants never contact their landlord.

### What people need

- **Tenants:** a warm, dry, healthy home, and help before they get ill, without feeling watched or judged.
- **Housing staff:** to know who to help first, what would help most, who should act and how urgently, and whether it worked.
- **Cloch:** to act within the new legal timescales, and to use limited money and staff where they make most difference.

### Things to keep in mind

- **Tenant privacy and consent.** Health information is especially sensitive.
- **Tight budgets.** Anything has to be affordable for a housing association.
- **Tenants who don't get in touch.** Waiting for reports won't reach them.
- **Information has to lead to action:** what to do, who does it, how urgently, and whether risk went down.

### Useful starting points

- Cloch already has an energy advice hub and a community support team.
- Open data on home energy performance (EPC certificates): statistics.gov.scot
